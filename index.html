Our Story

Built on Trust, Delivered with Excellence

Founded in August 2024 in Bangkok, AISTORAGE TECHNOLOGY (THAILAND) CO., LTD is a System Integrator focused on enterprise digital infrastructure.

We are dedicated to serving both Thai enterprises and Chinese companies expanding abroad — providing network, computing, storage, smart meeting, and IT modernization solutions with professional local delivery.

Our promise is simple: we don't just deliver equipment — we deliver your confidence in project success.
